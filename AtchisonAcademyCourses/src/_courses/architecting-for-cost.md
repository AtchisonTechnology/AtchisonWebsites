Welcome.

Most cost work starts at the bill and stops there. Tagging, rightsizing, commitments — real techniques, worth doing, and every one of them hits the same floor.

This module is about what sits under that floor.

You will read a real bill backwards, line item to decision. You will measure how much of your own spend you can actually explain. And you will draw the line between the cost work that belongs to finance and the cost work that only you can do.

Start with lesson 1.
