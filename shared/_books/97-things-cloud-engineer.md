## About the Book

*97 Things Every Cloud Engineer Should Know* brings together wisdom from dozens of experienced cloud practitioners in short, digestible essays. Lee Atchison contributed to this collection, sharing practical insights drawn from years of building and operating large-scale cloud systems.

The book covers a wide range of cloud engineering topics — from architecture and reliability to security, cost management, and organizational culture — making it a valuable reference for engineers and architects at every level.
