## About the Book

Identity is one of the most critical — and often underestimated — design challenges in modern applications. *Identity in Modern Applications* examines the full spectrum of identity management: authentication, authorization, session management, and the architectural choices that make or break the user experience and security posture of your application.

## What You'll Learn

- Modern authentication patterns including OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, and SAML
- Authorization models: RBAC, ABAC, and when to use each
- How to manage identity across microservices and distributed systems
- Practical guidance on common identity providers and integration approaches
