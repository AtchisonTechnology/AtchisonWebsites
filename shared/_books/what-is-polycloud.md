## About the Book

As organizations mature in their cloud journeys, many are moving beyond single-cloud strategies. *What is Polycloud?* explores the architectural models, trade-offs, and operational considerations for organizations that want to run workloads across multiple cloud providers.

## What You'll Learn

- The difference between multi-cloud and polycloud approaches
- When polycloud makes sense — and when it adds unnecessary complexity
- Architectural patterns for managing workloads across AWS, Azure, and GCP
- Data management, networking, and identity challenges in a polycloud environment
- Organizational and tooling considerations for polycloud operations
