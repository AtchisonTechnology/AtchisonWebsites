## Transform Your Company from Within

Co-authored by Lee Atchison and Ken Gavranovic — with 60+ combined years of experience — *Business Breakthrough 3.0* offers a proven methodology to clarify your operations, uncover limiting patterns, and build engaged, high-performing teams.

The book makes the case that data-driven decision-making consistently outperforms intuitive leadership, and gives you the frameworks to do it.

## Five Core Processes

1. **Mission, Vision, and Values** — Establish the foundation that aligns every decision in your organization
2. **Critical-Thinking Frameworks** — Use formal tools to make better, faster decisions under pressure
3. **Data Measurement and Interpretation** — Learn to measure what matters and read it correctly
4. **Organizational Structure Optimization** — Design teams and reporting lines that accelerate rather than obstruct
5. **Market Growth Strategy** — Leverage your positioning for sustainable sales growth
