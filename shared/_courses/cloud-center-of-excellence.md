## About This Course

Not every organization needs a Cloud Center of Excellence, and this course starts by helping you decide whether yours does before assuming the answer is yes.

For the organizations that do, it covers the organizational models a CCoE can take, who actually needs a seat at the table — inclusion and representation across the business, not just the cloud team — and how to measure whether it's working with data analytics rather than a feeling.

## What You'll Learn

- Deciding whether a Cloud Center of Excellence actually benefits your organization, before assuming the answer is yes
- Choosing the organizational model that fits how your company already works
- Including the right people — inclusion and representation across the business, not just the cloud team
- Measuring a CCoE's success with data analytics leadership will trust

## Who This Is For

IT leaders and cloud program owners deciding whether to stand up a Cloud Center of Excellence, and anyone already responsible for one who wants to check it against how this is actually meant to work.
