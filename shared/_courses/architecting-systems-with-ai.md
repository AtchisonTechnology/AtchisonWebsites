## About This Course

An AI component doesn't fail like the rest of your system. It doesn't throw a clean error, it doesn't retry the same way twice, and it can be confidently wrong instead of loudly broken. Most architecture practice was built for systems that fail more predictably than that.

This course is in development, and the ideas behind it get their first public airing in a free live webinar on Wednesday, September 30. Save your seat to get the earliest look at where the thinking is headed, and to help shape what the course becomes.

## Who This Is For

Architects and engineering leaders building systems that depend on AI components today, and need an architectural answer for the failure modes those components introduce.
