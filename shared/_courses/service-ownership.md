## About This Course

Ask who owns a given service at most organizations and you'll get a name, a team, or a shrug, depending on who you ask. Ask how critical that service actually is and the answer gets even less consistent. Both questions matter more as a system grows, and both get harder to answer honestly at the same time.

This course is in development, building on the service tier and STOSA (Single Team Oriented Service Architecture) thinking Lee has written about elsewhere. Take the free Service Ownership Diagnostic to see where your own organization's model has gaps, and help shape what the course covers.

## Who This Is For

Architects and engineering leaders responsible for how services get owned, tiered, and prioritized across an organization larger than one team can hold in their head.
