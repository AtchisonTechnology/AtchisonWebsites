## About This Course

Every organization wants to ship faster. Fewer have architecture that can absorb that speed without the failure rate climbing right along with it. Velocity and safety usually get treated as a trade-off instead of two things the same architecture can be designed for together.

This course is in early development. It isn't public yet, and there's nowhere to send you for it, but it will cover designing systems that stay safe under the pace teams are actually shipping at.

## Who This Is For

Architects and engineering leaders balancing delivery speed against system safety, and looking for an architectural answer instead of a process one.
