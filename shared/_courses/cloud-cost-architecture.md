## About This Course

Most teams treat cost as something that happens to an architecture after it's built, a number finance flags once a month rather than a decision made at design time. By the time it's visible, it's already load-bearing.

This course is in early development. It isn't public yet, and there's nowhere to send you for it, but it will cover architecting cost in from the start instead of discovering it later.

## Who This Is For

Architects and engineering leaders who need to own cost as part of the architecture itself, not just explain it after the fact.
