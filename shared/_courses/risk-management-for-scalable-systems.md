## About This Course

Most risk training treats risk as something to get rid of. Every complex system carries risk, so driving it to zero was never the job.

This course treats risk as a portfolio you manage on purpose. You build a risk matrix for your own systems, score it without inflating or hiding anything, and keep it alive as the system changes. The matrix is a real artifact, and a template comes with the course.

Then you use it. The matrix is what turns "we should really fix that" into an argument a roadmap owner will act on.

*Scalable Availability in Software Architecture* and *Cloud Architecture for Scalable Systems* cover related ground. Service tiers and internal SLAs from the availability course show up throughout this one. It stands on its own if the risk conversations are already landing on your desk.

The closing module covers what AI-enabled systems break about traditional risk practice.

## What You'll Learn

- Building a risk matrix for your own systems, and defending every row in it
- Scoring likelihood and severity without letting the two collapse into each other
- Finding the risks nobody has written down yet
- Keeping the matrix honest with review cadences and game days
- Choosing between mitigating a risk and removing it, and knowing which one you picked
- Designing service failures that are predictable, understandable, and reasonable
- Putting technical debt on the matrix with a score attached, so the argument to pay it down has evidence behind it
- Carrying a high-high list into a room full of executives and having it land
- Rating the risks that only exist once an AI component is in the system

## Who This Is For

Architects who own design decisions across several teams. Senior technical leaders responsible for systems they no longer write code for, and the operations leaders who carry the pager for other people's choices.

Developers growing toward technical leadership will get a head start on the part of the job nobody trains them for.

The course assumes you have a system with real risk in it and a manager to bring the high-high list to. It assumes nothing about formal risk, audit, or compliance experience, and there is no certification prep here.

## Course Structure

Six modules, about 12 hours total. Eighteen video lessons, eighteen companion readings, and a practice and a graded assignment in every module.

The modules move in order. Risk as an architectural responsibility, building the risk matrix, keeping the matrix alive, acting on risk, risk as a leadership tool, and risk in AI-enabled systems.
