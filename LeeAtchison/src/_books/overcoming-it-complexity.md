## About the Book

Every large IT organization faces a common enemy: complexity. As systems grow, teams scale, and cloud services multiply, the cognitive and operational burden of keeping everything running becomes a drag on innovation and agility.

*Overcoming IT Complexity* provides a practical roadmap for simplifying your IT environment — not by slowing down, but by making deliberate architectural choices that reduce accidental complexity and let your teams focus on what matters.

## What You'll Learn

- How to identify and classify the sources of complexity in your systems
- Strategies for reducing technical debt without stalling product development
- Cloud architecture patterns that prioritize simplicity and long-term maintainability
- Organizational approaches that reduce operational overhead and toil
- How to build a culture of simplicity that sustains itself as teams and systems grow

## Who This Book Is For

Technology leaders, architects, and senior engineers in organizations running complex cloud infrastructure who want practical frameworks for making their systems more manageable and their teams more effective.
