Background

Three Decades of Industry‑Shaping Experience

Lee Atchison is a CTO, Chief Software Architect, author, and thought leader specializing in cloud computing, application modernization, scalability, and high availability. With over three decades of experience, Lee has held executive and senior engineering roles at some of the world’s most innovative technology companies — including Amazon.com, AWS, New Relic, Blameless, and Hewlett Packard.

Lee is the co-founder of Product Genius, an AI-focused startup, and works directly with organizations as a Fractional Chief Architect consultant — helping enterprises modernize their applications, teams, and technology strategy across industries from finance to retail to sports.
